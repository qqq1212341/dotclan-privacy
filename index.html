3. 제3자 제공 · 처리 위탁

이용 통계와 광고 데이터는 위 목적으로 Google LLC(Firebase·AdMob)에 전송됩니다. Google 처리 방침: policies.google.com/privacy · 게스트 계정과 채팅은 Cloudflare, Inc. 의 서버(Workers·Durable Objects)에 저장됩니다. 그 밖의 제3자에게 넘기지 않습니다.

4. 광고

이 게임은 Google AdMob 광고를 보여 줍니다. 보상형 광고는 직접 눌렀을 때만 나오고, 전면 광고는 하루가 바뀐 뒤 처음 들어올 때 한 번 나올 수 있습니다. 광고는 개인 맞춤이 아닌 광고로 요청합니다. Google 광고 데이터 처리: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
기기 설정에서 광고 식별자를 초기화하거나 맞춤 광고를 끌 수 있습니다(Android: 설정 → Google → 광고).

5. 권한

사진·연락처·위치·마이크·카메라 같은 민감한 권한을 요청하지 않습니다. 인터넷 연결만 씁니다.

6. 아동

이 게임은 어린이를 대상으로 만들지 않았습니다. 만 13세 미만이 쓰는 것을 전제로 정보를 받지 않으며, 알게 되면 그 계정의 정보를 지웁니다.

7. 보관과 삭제

서버의 게스트 계정·진행 정보·채팅 글은 오래 접속하지 않은 계정부터 정리하며, 요청하시면 바로 지웁니다. 삭제를 원하시면 contact@dumbgamelab.com 으로 게임 안 닉네임(설정 창에 보입니다)을 적어 «계정 삭제» 를 요청해 주세요. 확인되는 대로 7일 안에 서버의 계정·진행 정보·채팅 글을 모두 지웁니다. 이용 통계는 Google Analytics 기본 보관 기간이 지나면 자동으로 지워집니다. 기기 안 저장분은 앱을 지우면 사라집니다.

Dot Clan — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-30 · Contact contact@dumbgamelab.com · Developer: Dumb Game Lab · 한국어

1. Summary

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, photos, contacts or location. There is no sign-up or login. On first launch the app creates a guest account from a random number generated on your device, and your game progress is stored on our server, because clan chat, rankings and sieges need one. We also collect anonymous usage statistics to fix the game, and we show ads.

2. What we collect

ItemDetailsPurpose
Guest account (our server)A random device-generated ID, the nickname you choose, game progress (level, gear, enhancement, currency, clan), last-seen timeResuming play; server features such as rankings, clans and sieges
Clan chat (our server)The chat messages you post to your clan with your nickname. Only the most recent messages per clan are kept; older ones are deleted automaticallyTalking with clan members
Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase / GA4)App open and close, progress steps (first kill, enhancement, floor clear, etc.), whether an ad was watched to the end, device model, OS version, approximate country, an app-instance ID (a random value that does not identify you), crash logsTo see where play drops off and fix the game
Ads (Google AdMob)Advertising ID, device and app information, ad impressions and clicksTo show ads and measure performance
Purchase recordsThe product ID you bought and the transaction confirmation value. We never receive card numbers or other payment details; Google Play and the App Store handle paymentTo deliver the purchase and prevent double grants
On-device storageGame progress and settings (app storage)Resuming play. Deleted when you uninstall the app

We do not identify individuals from this data and we do not sell it.

3. Third parties and processors

Usage statistics and ad data are sent to Google LLC (Firebase, AdMob) for the purposes above. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Guest accounts and chat are stored on servers of Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers, Durable Objects). We share data with no one else.

4. Ads

This game shows Google AdMob ads. Rewarded ads play only when you tap them, and an interstitial ad may play once, the first time you open the game after the day rolls over. Ads are requested as non-personalized. Google ad data handling: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Permissions

We do not request sensitive permissions such as photos, contacts, location, microphone or camera. We only use the internet connection.

6. Children

This game is not made for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13, and if we learn that we have, we delete that account's data.

7. Retention and deletion

Guest accounts, progress and chat messages on our server are cleaned up starting with long-inactive accounts, and deleted on request. To have yours deleted, email contact@dumbgamelab.com with your in-game nickname (shown in the Settings window) and the words "delete account". Once verified, we delete your account, progress and chat messages from the server within 7 days. Usage statistics are deleted automatically after Google Analytics' standard retention period. On-device data disappears when you uninstall the app.

8. Changes · 9. Contact

If how we handle data changes, we will announce it on this page first. Contact: contact@dumbgamelab.com
